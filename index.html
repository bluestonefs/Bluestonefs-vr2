Common questions

How much does commercial cleaning cost in Harrisonburg?

Pricing depends on the size of your space, the type of building, how often we clean, and the tasks on your checklist. After a free walkthrough, we send a written proposal with a clear monthly price.

Can you clean after business hours?

Yes. Most cleaning happens in the evening or on weekends so your team and customers are not disrupted. Daytime service is available if you prefer it.

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Yes. Our crews bring their own equipment and commercial-grade supplies. If you prefer specific products, tell us and we will add them to your checklist.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Harrisonburg and nearby communities in Rockingham County and the Shenandoah Valley, including Bridgewater, Dayton, Broadway, Staunton, and Waynesboro. Ask us if your town is not listed.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

We review the agreement terms with you in your written proposal, so you know exactly what to expect before we start.
